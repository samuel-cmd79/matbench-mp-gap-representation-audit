## Interpretation

- **Does BandCenter alone explain the major improvement?**
  No under the predeclared 50% descriptive threshold: adding BandCenter alone accounts for -1.0% of the observed Level-1 to Level-2 mean-MAE gain.
- **Fold consistency:** The BandCenter-difference direction was not consistent across all five folds.
- **Recommended paper wording:** “Structural-descriptor gain” can be retained descriptively because BandCenter did not produce a positive sequential mean-MAE gain, but the methods should still state that Level 2 contains BandCenter.
- **Boundary:** These are sequential gains under this feature-addition order, not strict causal contributions; BandCenter may interact with or be redundant with structural descriptors.
