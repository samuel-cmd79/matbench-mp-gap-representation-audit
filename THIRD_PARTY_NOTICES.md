## Source-informed ALIGNN Colab workflows

The following project-authored files are source-informed adaptations of the
public ALIGNN v2026.5.20 training, data-loading, configuration, and model APIs:

- `scripts/colab/v4_alignn_matbench_mp_gap_fixed_final_fi.py`
- `scripts/colab/v4_alignn_matbench_mp_gap_angle_zero_fold0.py`

They assemble project-specific MatBench-fold training and prediction
workflows, and the second adds the project's numerical angle-mask control.
They do not contain or replace the complete ALIGNN distribution. ALIGNN must
be installed separately, and its official NIST terms are reproduced below.
